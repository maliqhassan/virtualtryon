1aAssumptions, IA & navigation — read me first
Design brief · v1

AI Stylist

A wardrobe that thinks. The app answers one question every morning — what do I wear? — and everything else (closet, try-on, shopping) exists to make that answer better.

Decisions I made for you
Hero feature: Today's Outfit. One confident daily pick, generated overnight from closet + weather + calendar.
AI presence: named but quiet — Noor. No mascot, no chat bubbles on the home screen; it speaks through confidence scores and one line of reasoning per suggestion.
Navigation: 5 tabs — Today · Closet · Try-On (raised) · Shop · You. Chat lives as a persistent pill, reachable from any tab.
Monetization: free tier with soft limits (3 generations/day, 2 try-ons/week). Pro upsell appears at the moment of exhaustion, never before value.
Theme: light-first bone/ink palette, dark mode specified in the token table and shown on Try-On.
Try-on rendering: labeled generation placeholders with real progress states — I don't fake photoreal output. Drop your renders into the slots.
Information architecture
01  Today  → outfit · rate my fit · calendar · weather
02  Closet  → add · details · stats · laundry · packing
03  Try-On  → own clothes · store clothes · saved looks
04  Shop  → match feed · Eastern edit · product · wishlist
05  You  → style profile · settings · Pro · help
✦  Noor  → global chat overlay, context-aware
First-run journey
Splash→ 3× Onboarding→ Auth→ Scan (3 photos)→ Style profile→ Add 5 items→ First outfit

Time to first value target: under 3 minutes. Closet upload is batch-first (multi-select camera roll, AI tags in background) so the user is never asked to label anything.

1bDesign system — tokens, type, components

3 · Always an escape hatch: shuffle, edit, or "not this".

1eHandoff — responsive, accessibility, motion, Flutter
Responsive
Base 390dp. Below 360dp the closet grid drops to a single column and the outfit hero loses its side stack. 600dp+ (foldables, tablets) the tab bar becomes a NavigationRail and Closet → Details becomes a two-pane master/detail. All type uses MediaQuery.textScaler up to 200% — cards grow, never clip. Safe areas respected top and bottom; the glass tab bar adds viewPadding.bottom.
Accessibility
Body text passes 4.5:1 (#4E4841 on #F6F4F0 = 7.9:1); the clay accent is never used for body copy under 16px. Match quality is never colour-only — every badge carries its number and word. Hit targets ≥48dp. Every AI card exposes a semantics label combining score plus reasoning. Motion respects disableAnimations — the thinking glow becomes a static dot. RTL mirrored for Urdu, with Noto Nastaliq Urdu swapped in for headings.
Motion
Tab switch: fade-through 180ms. Outfit reveal: cards rise 10dp staggered 40ms, 520ms total. Try-on generation: three named steps, breathing glow at 1.5s, result cross-fades over 400ms. Sheets: 320ms easeOutCubic with a scrim fade. Score numbers count up over 700ms. Favourite tap: 120ms scale 0.9→1.06→1. Nothing bounces, nothing loops longer than 2s.
Flutter notes
Material 3 with a hand-authored ColorScheme from the token table (don't use fromSeed — it drifts the warm neutrals). Shell: StatefulShellRoute in go_router, five branches. Closet: SliverGrid + cached_network_image, Hero to Details. Glass: BackdropFilter only on the tab bar and try-on overlay — it's expensive. Type: google_fonts Manrope + Instrument Serif, bundled not fetched. Icons: material_symbols_icons rounded. Skeletons ship as real widgets, not a shimmer package wrapper.
Not designed yet — say the word
Full dark-mode pass on every screen · the lookbook as a scrollable editorial flow · saved-looks library · store-connection and brand onboarding.
